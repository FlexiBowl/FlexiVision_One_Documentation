# **Safety Information**

The following safety instructions, general precautions and regulations concerning handling and the operating environment must be closely observed to ensure the safety of personnel, the integrity of the product and the proper operation of the system.

```{warning}
**User Responsibility**

Compliance with all the safety regulations in this section is mandatory and the responsibility of the end user. Failure to do so may result in personal harm, damage to the equipment or jeopardise system operation.
```

---

## Operational safety

### *Integration with robotic systems*


```{warning} **Cell safety requirements**  

FlexiVision One works in close collaboration with third-party robotic systems. The user must ensure that the work area is equipped with all necessary safety measures set forth by the relevant standards.
```


```{warning} **Caution during operation**    

When operating the system, always take into account:

- Physical dimensions of the robot and the FlexiBowl®
- Trajectories and speed of robotic movements
- Possible unexpected situations (dropping parts, pick-up errors)
- Danger zones during FlexiBowl® handling phases
```

### *General precautions before interventions*


```{warning} **Power disconnection**  

Before carrying out any maintenance, modification or inspection work on the system, always make sure that:

- All power sources are disconnected (VisionController, FlexiBowl®, Camera, Illuminator)
- The pneumatic supply is discharged and disconnected (if present)
- Connection cables are physically disconnected
- The robot is in safety mode or completely switched off
```


```{warning} **Safety procedures**    

Do not rely solely on switches: use lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures when available.
```

### *Modifications and tampering*


```{warning} **Prohibition of unauthorised modifications**    

Never modify the product or its components without the express written consent of ARS S.r.l.
```

```{warning} **Consequences of modifications**    

Unauthorised modifications may:

- Cause system malfunctions
- Invalidate the warranty
- Create risks of injury, electric shock or fire
- Compromise product safety certifications
```

## Permitted environmental conditions

The system is designed for **indoor** use **only**, protected against the elements and aggressive agents. The environment must not be ATEX classified.

````{list-table}
:header-rows: 1
:widths: 30 70

* - Parameter
  - Permissible limit
* - **Ambient temperature**
  - 5 ÷ 40 °C
* - **Relative humidity**
  - 5 – 90% (non-condensing), max. variation 0.005 p.u./h
* - **Ambient lighting**
  - Neon light
* - **Dust**
  - Absence of excessive, abrasive or combustible dust
* - **Vapours and gases**
  - Absence of corrosive fumes, oily vapours, explosive mixtures
* - **Vibrations and shocks**
  - Absence of abnormal vibrations, impact or shocks
* - **Thermal fluctuations**
  - Maximum 5K/h
* - **Other exclusions**
  - Salty air, bad weather, nuclear radiation, unusual storage conditions
````

## Environmental conditions and protection

### *Protection from liquids*


```{warning} **Risk of contact with liquids**    

Do not use the product in environments where the VisionController, camera or other electronic components may come into contact with:

- Water drops or splashes
- Oils, lubricants or other industrial liquids
- Condensate or excessive moisture
- Conductive dusts
```

```{note} **Solutions for critical environments**    

If the system needs to operate in environments where liquids are present, provide adequate protection (IP65 or higher enclosures) and consult the ARS Technical Service for customised solutions.
```

### *Operating temperatures*


```{warning} **Hot surfaces - Maximum temperatures**    

Under intensive use or hot environments, some system components can reach high temperatures:

- VisionController: up to 50°C on exterior surfaces
- LED illuminator: up to 40°C on front surface
- Industrial camera: up to 50°C on metal housing
```


```{warning} **Customer's responsibility**    

It is the customer's responsibility to:

- Record thermal risks in the risk assessment
- Train personnel on procedures to avoid accidental contact
- Provide warning signs where necessary
- Ensure adequate ventilation of components
```

### *Environmental installation and storage conditions*

```{note} **Environmental requirements - Reference table**    

To ensure durability and reliability, the VisionController and the camera must be used and stored under the following conditions:

| Parameter | Operating conditions | Storage conditions |
|-----------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| **Temperature** | +5°C ÷ +40°C | +5°C ÷ +40°C |
| **Relative humidity** | <90% (non-condensing) | <90% (non-condensing) |
```

```{note} **Additional precautions for the environment**    

To preserve the integrity of the components:

- Do not expose to direct sunlight
- Protect against excessive vibration during storage
- Keep in a dry and dust-free place
- The camera is sensitive to mechanical shocks: handle with care
```

## Transport and handling

### *Reception and inspection*

```{note} **Inspection on arrival**    

Upon receipt of the product, before signing the delivery note:

1. **External inspection of packaging**: Check the integrity of the box and outer packaging. Check for any signs of impact, crushing or wetting.
2. **Content check**: Compare the contents with the delivery note. Check that all ordered components are present.
```

```{note} **In the event of damage or discrepancies**    

If problems are encountered:

- DO NOT sign the receipt as "compliant"
- Note down the damage on the transport document
- Take pictures of any obvious damage
- Contact ARS service immediately:
  [service@arsautomation.com](mailto:service@arsautomation.com)
  [us.service@arsautomation.com](mailto:us.service@arsautomation.com) if writing from America
```

### *Handling and storage*

To prevent damage during transport and storage:

```{tip}
**During transport:**
- Always move the packaging in an upright position (follow the "TOP" arrows on the packaging)
- Do not drop or impact the package
- Use trolleys or pallet trucks suitable for the weight
- Avoid sudden temperature changes
```

```{tip}
**During storage:**
- Store in a dry, covered place
- Do not stack other loads on top of the packaging
- Do not climb on or lean against the packaging
- Comply with the environmental conditions indicated in the table above
```

```{tip}
**During unpacking:**
- Open carefully so as not to damage the internal components
- Keep the original packaging for any returns or future transport
- Verify that all accessories and documentation are present
```
